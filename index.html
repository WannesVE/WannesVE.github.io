Sleep Relax Studio

Original sleep and meditation music for YouTube.

Sleep Relax Studio is a small private tool used by one independent creator to publish long-form, original sleep and meditation music on their own YouTube channel.

The tool composes the music and calm visuals itself, uploads each finished video to the creator's own channel as private through the YouTube Data API, and the creator reviews every video manually in YouTube Studio before publishing it.

It is not a public application: it has a single user and does not access any other person's account or data.

Uses YouTube API Services

This application uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
